形声 ShapeSoundShapeSound

在纸上画线，播放头扫过就发声：线越高音越高，颜色就是乐器。16 种实时合成音色、11 首示例曲，还能导入谱子、把照片描成会响的画。Draw lines on paper and a playhead turns them into sound: height is pitch, colour is instrument. 16 synthesised instruments, 11 demo songs, score import and photo-to-line tracing.

怎么玩How to play

  • 竖直位置就是音高：横线是长音，斜线是琶音，竖线是和弦。Vertical position is pitch: horizontal lines hold, diagonals arpeggiate, verticals make chords.
  • 16 种颜色对应 16 种乐器，全部用 Web Audio 实时合成。16 colours map to 16 instruments, all synthesised live with the Web Audio API.
  • 12 个调 × 11 种音阶，选对音阶，怎么画都和谐。12 keys × 11 scales — pick a scale and anything you draw sounds right.
  • 支持导入简谱、MusicXML、MIDI，或者把照片自动描成线条。Import numbered notation, MusicXML or MIDI, or trace a photo into lines.
  • 可以导出 WAV / MIDI，也能接 MIDI 键盘弹奏。Export WAV / MIDI, or play along on a MIDI keyboard.
