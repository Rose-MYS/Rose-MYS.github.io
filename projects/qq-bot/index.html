QQ 机器人「小瓜」Xiaogua QQ Bot

接入 DeepSeek 等大模型的 QQ 群聊机器人：有人格、有情绪、有长期记忆，会看图、会斗图，还能自己学新梗、写周记，掉线了能自愈。A QQ group-chat bot powered by DeepSeek and other LLMs — with personas, moods and long-term memory. It reads images, trades memes, picks up new slang, writes a weekly diary and recovers from disconnects on its own.

    小瓜会做什么What Xiaogua can do

    • 人格系统：本体人格加多套预设，可以模仿、切换人格。Personas: a core persona plus presets; it can switch or imitate styles.
    • 情绪与节奏：七种情绪，先判断该不该接话，再加冷却和每小时上限，不刷屏。Mood & pacing: seven moods and a “should I reply?” gate with cooldowns and hourly caps, so it never spams.
    • 长期记忆：按人、群、全局三层记忆，聊天中自动提炼。Long-term memory: per-person, per-group and global memories distilled from chats.
    • 看图和斗图：能看懂群友发的图片；两百多张带语义标签的表情包，可以自主斗图。Images & memes: understands pictures people send and picks from 200+ tagged memes on its own.
    • 自我进化：定时去 B 站学新梗，感知群里的氛围，调整说话口味。Self-evolution: periodically learns new slang from Bilibili and adapts to each group’s vibe.
    • 约定追踪和周记：记住聊天里的约定，到点提醒；每周日自动写一篇周记。Follow-ups & diary: remembers promises made in chat and checks back; writes a weekly diary every Sunday.
    • 作息与自愈：按作息睡觉、起床；看护脚本自动处理掉线、重连和扫码。Routine & self-healing: keeps a sleep schedule; a watchdog handles disconnects, reconnection and QR re-login.
    • 控制台：网页面板，查看各插件状态，一键暂停或恢复。Dashboard: a web panel showing every plugin’s status, with one-click pause and resume.

    机器人涉及账号和 API 密钥，代码不公开。The bot involves account credentials and API keys, so the code is not public.
