模拟指挥Gesture Driven Ensemble

东南大学第一届新生 AI 创客大赛参赛作品。站在摄像头前，用双手像指挥家一样控制一支乐队的速度与音量。Our entry for the 1st SEU Freshman AI Maker Competition. Stand in front of a camera and conduct a band with your hands — tempo and volume follow your gestures.

这是什么What it is

参与者站在摄像头前，像指挥家一样挥动双手：一只手控制乐队的速度，另一只手控制整体音量。动作不是装饰，而是直接改变音乐的节奏和动态。Participants stand in front of a camera and conduct with both hands — one hand sets the tempo, the other the overall volume. The gestures are not decoration; they directly reshape the music.

比赛还在进行中，更多细节会在比赛结束后补充。The competition is still running — more details will follow once it ends.
