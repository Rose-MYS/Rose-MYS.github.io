AI + 学习AI & learning2026-11-26

能不能“蒸馏”一位老师？Could AI “distill” a teacher?

把本校教材喂给 AI 之后，我发现它讲得和老师差不多。下一步，能不能让 AI 学会一位老师的讲法？After feeding my university’s own textbooks to an AI, I found it explained things about as well as my lectures. Could it go one step further and learn how a particular teacher teaches?

本页目录On this page

    我做过的事：让 AI 照着本校教材讲What I did: AI that teaches from my own textbooks

    上大学后我开始用 AI 辅助学习，摸索出一个做法：把本校的教材和习题喂给 AI，让它照着这些材料给我讲。Since starting university I’ve been using AI to help me study, and I settled on one habit: give the AI my university’s own textbooks and problem sets, and have it explain from those materials.

    我主要在工科数学分析、线性代数和 C++ 这几门课上这样用。对我来说，讲解效果和老师上课差不多，效率却高了很多。I mostly do this for Engineering Mathematical Analysis, Linear Algebra and C++. For me, the explanations are about as good as lectures, and I learn much faster.

    我觉得 AI 有三个优点：I see three advantages:

    1. 讲的就是我们课上的东西。因为喂的是本校材料，它用的符号、例题、题型都和课堂一致，不会讲成网上的通用版本。It teaches exactly what our course covers. Because it works from our own materials, its notation, examples and problem types match the class instead of a generic internet version.
    2. 能完整展示最优的做题思路。一道题从哪里入手、为什么这样想、哪一步最关键，它都能讲清楚。It can lay out the best line of reasoning in full. Where to start, why to think that way, which step matters most — it explains all of it.
    3. 效率高。哪里不懂问哪里，懂了就跳过，不用跟着全班的进度走。以线性代数里的最小二乘法为例，AI 可以这样讲：先从问题出发——方程组 Ax = b 没有解时，退而求其次，找一个让误差 ‖Ax − b‖ 最小的 x；再用几何直观——Ax 只能落在 A 的列空间里，离 b 最近的点就是 b 在列空间上的投影，所以误差 b − Ax 要和 A 的每一列都垂直，由此自然推出正规方程 AᵀAx = Aᵀb；最后拿本校习题里的数据走一遍完整计算。哪一步没懂，就停在那一步追问。It’s efficient. I ask about what I don’t get and skip what I do, instead of following the whole class’s pace. Take least squares in linear algebra. An AI can start from the problem — when Ax = b has no solution, settle for the x that makes the error ‖Ax − b‖ as small as possible; then give the geometric picture — Ax can only lie in the column space of A, the closest point to b is its projection onto that space, so the error b − Ax must be perpendicular to every column of A, which leads straight to the normal equations AᵀAx = Aᵀb; and finally work through a full calculation with numbers from our own problem sets. Whenever a step is unclear, I stop there and ask.

    我还发现，讲解效果主要受两样东西影响：模型本身，和给模型预设的全局提示词（system prompt）。我用过 DeepSeek V4.1 Flash、通义千问和 Gemini 等模型。不同模型的教学风格不一样，相应的效果也会有所不同。I also found that two things matter most: the model itself, and the system prompt you give it. I’ve used DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, Qwen and Gemini, among others. Different models have different teaching styles, and the results differ accordingly.

    我的设想：“蒸馏”一位老师My idea: “distilling” a teacher

    在 AI 领域，“蒸馏”指用一个强大的模型去教一个小模型，让小模型学会它的本事。我借用这个词，提出一个还没验证的设想：In AI, “distillation” means using a strong model to teach a smaller one until the smaller one picks up its skills. I’m borrowing the word for an idea I haven’t tested yet:

    如果不只喂教材，而是把一门课老师的课件、讲义、板书、往年题都交给 AI，让它学会这门课的重点和这位老师的讲法，能不能得到一个讲课水平接近这位老师的“AI 老师”？What if, instead of just the textbook, we gave the AI a teacher’s slides, notes, board work and past exams, so it learned that course’s emphasis and that teacher’s way of explaining? Would we get an “AI teacher” that explains nearly as well as the real one?

    我认为对一部分课程有可能做到，理由是：I think it may work for some courses, because:

    • 材料决定上限。通用 AI 什么都懂一点，但不知道你们老师强调什么、考试怎么考。我喂本校教材时已经看到了这种差别，喂进老师自己的材料，效果可能更好。Materials set the ceiling. A general AI knows a bit of everything but not what your teacher stresses or how exams are set. I already saw the difference when I used our own textbooks; the teacher’s own materials might work even better.
    • 模型和提示词决定下限。模型越强、提示词越清楚，越能从材料里抓住重点，越少讲错。The model and prompt set the floor. The stronger the model and the clearer the prompt, the better it picks out what matters and the fewer mistakes it makes.

    但这只是设想。我打算之后找一门课实际试一试，结果另写一篇。But it’s only an idea for now. I plan to try it on one course and write up the results separately.

    研究怎么说What the research says

    近两年几项比较严谨的随机对照实验，和我的体验方向一致：Several fairly rigorous randomised controlled trials from the past two years point the same way as my experience:

    • 哈佛大学物理课（194 名本科生）：同样的内容，跟专门设计过的 AI 导师学的学生，比上真人主动学习课的学生学到的更多、花的时间更少。Harvard physics (194 undergraduates): with the same content, students who learned from a purpose-built AI tutor learned more, in less time, than students in an in-person active-learning class.
    • 英国中学数学（Google DeepMind × Eedi，1179 名学生）：在当场帮学生纠正错误这件事上，AI 导师和专家级真人导师效果相当。UK secondary maths (Google DeepMind × Eedi, 1,179 students): at helping students fix mistakes in the moment, the AI tutor matched expert human tutors.

    这些研究里的 AI 都配了教学设计和课程材料，而不是直接打开聊天窗口就用。这也支持我的判断：用好 AI 的关键，在于你给它什么。In these studies the AI came with teaching design and course materials — nobody just opened a chat window. That supports my view: the key to using AI well is what you give it.

    要注意的地方Things to watch out for

    1. “看懂了”不等于“学会了”1. Understanding it isn’t the same as learning it

    AI 能把最优思路完整讲出来，这既是优点也是陷阱。宾夕法尼亚大学在土耳其做过一个近千人的实验：用普通 ChatGPT 做练习的学生，练习成绩高了 48%；可拿走 AI 一考试，反而比从没用过的同学低 17%。而用“只给提示、不给答案”的 AI 导师的学生，考试成绩没有变差。An AI that lays out the full solution is both a strength and a trap. In a University of Pennsylvania study with nearly 1,000 high-school students in Turkey, students who practised with plain ChatGPT scored 48% higher during practice — but once the AI was taken away, they scored 17% lower on the exam than students who never had it. Students using an AI tutor that gave hints instead of answers did no worse.

    我的体会是：不自己独立动手做题，就永远无法验证自己是否真的懂了。My own takeaway: unless you solve problems on your own, you can never check whether you truly understand.

    比较稳妥的用法是：先自己做，做不出来再要提示，最后才看完整思路；看完后关掉 AI，独立做一道同类题。A safer routine: try it yourself first, ask for a hint only when stuck, look at the full solution last — then close the AI and solve a similar problem on your own.

    2. AI 还是会讲错2. AI still makes mistakes

    即使喂了教材，AI 偶尔也会算错或讲偏。遇到和课本、老师说法不一致的地方，要回到原始材料核对。Even with the textbook, AI sometimes miscalculates or drifts off. When it disagrees with the book or the teacher, go back to the original material and check.

    3. 尊重老师的劳动3. Respect teachers’ work

    课件、讲义和习题是老师和学校的成果。如果真去“蒸馏”一位老师，产物只应该用于自己学习，不应该公开传播老师的材料。Slides, notes and problem sets are the work of teachers and the university. If you do “distill” a teacher, keep the result for your own study and don’t share the teacher’s materials publicly.

    4. 这只是一个学生的体验4. This is one student’s experience

    “效果和老师讲课差不多”是我个人的感受；“蒸馏老师”更是还没验证的设想。不同课程、不同学生可能差别很大。“About as good as lectures” is my personal impression, and “distilling a teacher” is still an untested idea. Results may vary a lot across courses and students.

    更大的意义：教培老师会被替代吗？The bigger picture: will tutoring teachers be replaced?

    如果大学课程的讲解都能由 AI 照着教材完成，补习班老师的讲解当然也可以。那补习班会消失吗？我的看法是不会那么快，因为家长花的钱，很大一部分不是为讲解付的：If AI can explain university courses from the textbook, it can certainly do what after-school tutors do. So will tutoring centres disappear? I don’t think so, not soon — because much of what parents pay for isn’t the explaining:

    • 据媒体报道，教培行业收入差距极大：线下主讲月薪普遍一两万元，只有少数被机构打造成“明星主讲”的老师，年薪达到几十万甚至上百万。家长愿意为明星老师付高价，买的主要是信任。According to press reports, pay in China’s tutoring industry varies enormously: most in-person lead teachers earn ¥10,000–20,000 a month, and only the few that companies build into “star teachers” earn hundreds of thousands or even millions a year. What parents pay a premium for is mainly trust.
    • 督学、陪伴、升学信息，这些 AI 也还做不好。Keeping students on track, being there for them, and admissions know-how are things AI still doesn’t do well.
    • 教培公司自己已经在用 AI 了，比如“名师主讲 + 二讲服务 + AI 伴学”的模式。AI 先替代的是低薪的辅导岗位，名师反而能覆盖更多学生。Tutoring companies already use AI themselves, e.g. a “star lecturer + assistant teachers + AI study companion” model. AI replaces the low-paid support roles first, while star teachers reach even more students.

    所以更准确的说法是：讲解这项劳动正在被 AI 替代；对自律的学生来说，最大的变化是终于可以不为它付高价了。So a more accurate way to put it: the work of explaining is being taken over by AI; for self-motivated students, the biggest change is that they no longer have to pay a high price for it.

    如果你也想试试If you want to try it

    1. 收集材料：教材电子版、老师给的习题。Collect materials: the textbook in digital form and the problem sets your teacher gives you.
    2. 选一个好模型，最好用支持上传文件的工具。Pick a good model, ideally in a tool that lets you upload files.
    3. 写好全局提示词（导师设定）。这一步对效果影响很大，例如：Write a good system prompt (tutor setup). This makes a big difference, for example:

    你是《【课程名】》的助教。只依据我上传的教材和习题讲解，符号和方法与教材保持一致；材料里没有的内容要明确说明。讲题时先问我做到哪一步，每次只给一个提示；我做完后，再展示完整的最优思路，并指出关键的一步。You are a teaching assistant for [course name]. Explain only from the textbook and problem sets I upload, keeping the same notation and methods; say clearly when something isn’t in the materials. When working through a problem, first ask how far I’ve got and give one hint at a time; after I finish, show the full best solution and point out the key step.

    1. 先做后问，学完自测：关掉 AI，独立做一道同类题。Try first, ask later, then test yourself: close the AI and solve a similar problem on your own.
