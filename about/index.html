关于我About me

Rose，东南大学计算机专业大一学生。Rose — a first-year computer science student at Southeast University, Nanjing.

我在做什么What I do

我喜欢把抽象的东西变得能看、能听、能玩：把线条变成音乐，把手势变成指挥，把变流器的控制策略变成仿真波形。I like making abstract things visible, audible and playable — lines into music, gestures into conducting, converter control strategies into simulation waveforms.

感兴趣的方向Interests

  • 电力电子与控制：MATLAB / Simulink 建模与仿真Power electronics and control: modelling and simulation in MATLAB / Simulink
  • Web 音频与创意编程：Web Audio API、实时合成、可视化Web audio and creative coding: Web Audio API, real-time synthesis, visualisation
  • 人机交互：用摄像头和手势控制声音Human–computer interaction: controlling sound with cameras and gestures
  • 网页游戏：Three.js 3D 小游戏Web games: small 3D games with Three.js
