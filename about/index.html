关于我About me

Rose，东南大学计算机专业大一学生。Rose — a first-year computer science student at Southeast University, Nanjing.

我在做什么What I do

我喜欢把抽象的东西变得能看、能听、能玩：把线条变成音乐，把手势变成指挥，把录音变成乐谱，把变流器的控制策略变成仿真模型。I like making abstract things visible, audible and playable — lines into music, gestures into conducting, recordings into sheet music, converter control into simulation models.

  • 人机交互：用摄像头和手势控制声音Human–computer interaction: controlling sound with cameras and gestures
